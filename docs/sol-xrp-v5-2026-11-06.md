## What v4 trades showed and what changed

**SOL:** v4 had 16 closed trades and seven wins. One development trade, entry index
2831, remained open through a favorable move and ultimately stopped out for
**−14.83990199 USDT** at index 2846. Another, entry 1344, gave back much of a favorable
move and exited for +8.46709844 USDT. These motivated two separate hypotheses:
delayed trailing protection, and a nearer target when entry volatility is low.

The selected SOL v5 keeps v4's entries, rising MA200 filter, cost gate and 2.5 ATR stop.
It chooses **4 ATR profit target when completed signal ATR14/close ≤0.8%; otherwise
5 ATR**. The signal's target is fixed before the next-open fill. Trailing candidates
were tested, retained and rejected by the development ranking; the winning profile
does not use trailing stops.

Entry 2831 now closes at target at index 2838 for **+17.04650968 USDT**, a direct
improvement of **31.88641167 USDT**. The other eight earlier trades keep their v4
outcomes. Later trades retain their exit timing and rules, with small amount changes
because the full-period account now has more equity. SOL still loses on several trades:
it has 16 closed trades and eight wins, not an all-winning strategy.

**XRP:** v4 had four closed trades, all winners. The second entry (1000) hit the
3.5 ATR target at index 1012; that exit candle's high extended far beyond the target.
OHLC cannot establish the price sequence after the original exit. This motivated a
conditional extension based on the **already completed signal candle**, rather than
using the future high to decide whether to hold.

Selected XRP v5 keeps the 2 ATR stop, fresh MA20/50 crossover, rising MA200, range
0.8–2 ATR, upper-half close and cost gate. It chooses **6 ATR target when signal
range/ATR is 1.5–1.8 inclusive; otherwise 3.5 ATR**. Entry 1000's signal range/ATR is
approximately 1.65934, so it qualifies. It exits in the same index-1012 candle for
**+43.98384393 USDT**, versus v4's **+24.615162**, improving that trade by
**19.36868193 USDT**. The later two trades keep their timing and target rules; small
profit differences are compounding. XRP still has only four closed trades/four wins,
which is a very small sample.

Entry indices are zero-based in these frozen snapshots. The **Why & v5 profiles** tab
shows all matched closed-trade amounts and exits; **Trade explorer** opens every
