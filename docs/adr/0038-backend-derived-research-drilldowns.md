# ADR 0038: Reconstructible research drilldowns

2026-11-03 · Module 14 Iteration 2 · Accepted

Graphs render actual provenance edges and active contradictions from selected immutable
news snapshots. Original and corrected knowledge remain separately selectable. The
archive is explicitly separate from paper evidence unless exact identifiers bind them.
Verified native event-study artifacts supply descriptive response tables with missing
windows; they do not imply causation or an executable profit probability.

Four-arm performance, paired uncertainty, drawdown, fees, stress costs, execution traces,
complete-day arm correlations and frozen risk/configuration parameters come from the API.
Missing cross-asset panels and producer bundles remain N/A. Curve buckets preserve
missing samples as gaps; the raw paginated equity API retains reconstruction inputs.
JSON/CSV/Markdown exports bind the immutable evaluation manifest. Keyboard focus is
trapped/restored in the evidence drawer; Escape closes it.
