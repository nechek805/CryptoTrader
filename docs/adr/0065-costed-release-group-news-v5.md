# ADR 0065: Frozen-parent release-category news V5

Status: accepted, 2026-11-08.

V2's ADA, AVAX and ETH profits arise from token-specific recurring macro
category reactions. V4's signed features and stricter evidence requirements
abstain. Neither outcome establishes a tradable news edge, especially given
V2's losses under one-hour delay.

Add a separate descriptive V5 experiment with three horizons, training-only
directions, costed per-category validation and a concentration penalty. Keep
eight training samples, twice estimated costs, three category validation trades
across two months and five combined validation trades. Explicitly report the
zero uncertainty margin and all adaptive group checks; never claim V4 confidence.
Use the same policy for all tokens and retain negative outcomes/cash fallback.

Use immutable V4 corpus/market manifests by reference and copy every old model
object exactly. New tables and versioned BullMQ/API dispatch isolate storage;
missing market chunks must fail without downloads. Preserve original research
reports, source configurations, collectors and financial services.

Consequences: comparisons have common data/costs and auditable attribution, but
the final period is already inspected and historical availability remains
unverified. V5 is descriptive only and may underperform V2. Real-news forward
qualification requires separately captured point-in-time evidence.

See [protocol and trade analysis](../news-strategy-v5.md).
