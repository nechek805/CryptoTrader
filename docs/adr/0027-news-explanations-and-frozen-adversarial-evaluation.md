# ADR 0027: Grounded news explanations and frozen adversarial evaluation

Status: Accepted. Date: 2026-11-02. Scope: Module 10 Iteration 3.

Expose structured descriptions of exact known news prefixes and canonical provider
decisions. Preserve claims, origin attribution, opposing/retracted evidence, event age,
publication/receipt clocks, comparison cohorts, costs and abstention reasons. Treat all
source text as quoted data and escape active Markdown/HTML in human-readable reports.
The public frame wrapper regenerates the decision; durable artifacts replay all semantics.
No source string can execute a command or change policy. Pattern-based quarantine is
additional defense and does not claim complete instruction detection.

Extend existing synthetic fixture recipes with independent adversarial cases and a later
TEST cohort while preserving original default fixture behavior. Freeze model availability,
opportunity IDs, horizon, baselines, costs and evaluation before TEST. Reuse exact-close
outcome gates and reject fitting-lineage/price reuse or malformed opportunity bindings.
Keep TEST prices entirely outside the news producer. No refitting or choosing a favorable
baseline/horizon after seeing TEST is allowed.

Compare paired price markouts against cash and always-long on the same declared
news-conditioned opportunity set. Do not invent fills, executable SHORTs, portfolio
equity or net PnL. UTC-day/lineage/window/price blocks prevent naive sample inflation;
conditional mean intervals use two-comparison nominal multiplicity. Retain missing
coverage and negative evidence. Price false positives and missed events are hindsight
outcomes, not factual truth assessments or permission to override abstention.

Archive every exact extraction identity, known prompt/model recipe and per-article cost.
Unknown costs/prompts remain unknown; retain the deterministic no-LLM fallback. Publish
atomic immutable bundles containing prefix snapshots, explanations, evaluation, journal,
extraction archive and Module 11 handoff. Regenerate every indexed byte during semantic
verification. Existing Module 06–10 artifact formats and SQL remain unchanged.

The default synthetic evaluation retains four false positives and six missed-event
cases. Its paired intervals cross zero; no positive real incremental edge, authenticated
preregistration, trading readiness or promotion is claimed. Module 11 receives structural
provider ports, default HOLD and explicit eligibility/execution warnings. Fusion, general
risk and paper-broker work remain separate subsequent iterations.
