# ADR 0042: Immutable after-action assessment

2026-11-03 · Module 15 Iteration 3 · Accepted

trial-assessment.v1 binds the exact run, protocol, annotation chain and canonical
four-arm evaluation. It retains cash and per-asset passive endpoints, paired uncertainty,
calendar/asset decompositions, modeled stop/target outcomes, cost/latency stresses,
corroboration-delay missingness, daily/weekly QA and source incidents. Continuous and
interrupted analyses remain separate; downtime has no invented prices or profits.
Recommendations are CONTINUE_FORWARD_TEST, REVISE_AND_RESET, or STOP. Actual public
receipt time, full elapsed duration, sufficient evidence, coverage, integrity and
robustness are required before even continuing research. No real-money permission exists.

Content-addressed trial bundles include manifest, exact raw run, assessment, findings and
a verified nested evaluation bundle. Semantic verification rebuilds the documents and
checks inventory/hashes. Post-hoc hypotheses are a ranked future backlog and cannot
retroactively amend the protocol. Only already semantically rebuilt, deeply frozen
in-memory reports may reuse verification work; caller-frozen inputs are never trusted.
